Try this, in order
  • 1. Swipe up on the lock screen. It follows your finger.
  • 2. Open Works. Then swipe up from the bottom edge to go home. The app shrinks back into its icon.
  • 3. Swipe up and hold in an app for the switcher. Fling a card up to kill it.
  • 4. Drag down from the top for notifications. Top right corner for Control Center.
  • 5. Grab any moving surface mid flight. Everything is interruptible.
  • 6. Open Terminal, type teta-fetch. Open Settings to watch the live FPS meter.

On desktop, test mouse drags here first. The real gate is on your phone: open this file or its hosted copy in mobile Chrome and Safari and run the same six steps.
